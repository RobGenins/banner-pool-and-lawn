About Banner

One family. One standard. Twenty years of backyards done right.

Banner Pool and Lawn, LLC is a family owned and operated company based in North Richland Hills, Texas. Owner Brian Banner started the business over twenty years ago with a simple idea: take care of a small number of homes the way you'd take care of your own, and let the work speak for itself.

It worked. Today the company looks after about twenty-five clients — and every single one of them came to Banner by referral. Most have been with Brian for over fifteen years. There has never been a sales pitch, a call center, or a rotating crew. Just steady, personal care, week after week, season after season.

Because the client list is kept deliberately small, every pool and every lawn gets real attention — the kind you can't give when you're racing through forty stops a day.

— Brian Banner, Owner
